## svgo

- Source: https://github.com/svg/svgo
- License: MIT, Copyright (c) Kir Belevich
- Used in:
  - `svgo/`: a MoonBit port of svgo's preset-default plugins. Plugin behavior
    follows svgo's source, and some routines are direct translations, such as
    the arc-to-curve conversion (`a2c`) in `svgo/plugins/structure.mbt`. The
    npm package (`svgo.wasm`, `cli.mjs`) and the native CLI are compiled from
    this code.
  - `svgo/plugins/svg_tables.mbt`: generated by `scripts/gen-svg-tables.mjs`
    from svgo's `plugins/_collections.js`.
  - `svgo/plugins/fixtures/upstream/`: svgo's plugin test cases, copied
    verbatim (source commit in `NOTICE.md` there);
    `svgo/plugins/fixtures_test.mbt` is generated from them.
